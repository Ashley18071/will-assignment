AFRICAN FOODS COMPANY – WEBSITE PROJECT
=======================================

FILES
- index.html: website content and page structure
- styles.css: layout, colours and responsive design
- script.js: product filtering, mobile navigation and demo basket

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Keep all three files together in the same folder.
3. Double-click index.html, or right-click it and choose Open with > Chrome.
4. An internet connection is needed for the online photos and Google Fonts.

CUSTOMISE BEFORE SUBMISSION OR PUBLISHING
- Replace the sample products, descriptions and prices with information approved for your project.
- Replace add-company-email@example.com in index.html and script.js with a real company email address.
- Add the real company address, telephone number, delivery/collection details and social media links.
- Check that all product photos are suitable for your assignment/business.

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end student website concept. The products and prices are examples only.
The basket is a demonstration and does not take payments, save orders, check stock or send orders to a company system.
The contact form opens the visitor's email application; it does not store messages.
For a live online shop, the company would need a secure backend, verified product/stock data,
payment integration, privacy policy and tested order fulfilment process.
